## B1 — Pick a generation from run-16 {#b1}
_2026-08-06 · **needs the user** — the plan designates this pick as theirs ([F1 of the
predecessor](../2026-08-02-lora-beat-e07/forks.md#f1): the user's eyes decide)_

**What blocks:** [P5](todo.md)'s `e07` head-to-head and the ship/no-ship call both name "the chosen
generation".

**What is on the table:** all 12 of run-16 are saved as `rd_styl_anima_r16_g01…g12`.

**My recommendation: g08.** It is the generation both thesis tests were run at and both passed —
the anteater gained facial detail from the base, the cat became a recognisable cat, and both kept
colour. Run-16 is unusually stable across g8/g10/g12, so the choice matters less here than in any
previous run; g10 would also be defensible.

**Against g10+:** the snake degrades to a flat green disc by g10, and while run-16 is stable
overall, nothing improves after g8 that I can see.
